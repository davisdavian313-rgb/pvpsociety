Helper Duties & Requirements

Helpers are the frontline of our community. Review the criteria and responsibilities below.

  • Core Responsibilities: Assist players with general questions, report bugs/issues to administration, and support the moderation team when needed.
  • Promotion Opportunity: Staff members who consistently demonstrate dedication, reliability, and great performance are eligible for promotion.
  • Activity & Communication: Active presence in-game and on Discord with a calm, professional attitude toward all members.
  • Clean Record: No recent rule infractions or bans across our community.
